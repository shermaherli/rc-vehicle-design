# Student-Oriented RC Vehicle Design

Development of a functional RC vehicle using QFD and 3D modeling, with hands-on manufacturing of its main components.

## My Contribution
- 3D modeling and design validation in Blender based on QFD requirements.
- Fabrication of the chassis using CNC machining.
- Casting of the rims and fabrication of molds for the rubber tires.
- Construction of the fiberglass bodywork.

## Tools & Technologies
- **Software:** Blender, CAD tools.
- **Manufacturing:** CNC machining, casting of rims, fiberglass, rubber molding.
- **Methodologies:** QFD (Quality Function Deployment).

## Results
- Successfully assembled a functional vehicle prototype.
- Validated the design and manufacturing process.
- Presented the project at a science fair and poster competition.

## What I Learned
This project gave me hands-on experience in integrating design, simulation, and manufacturing. I learned to work with composite materials, casting processes, and CNC machining, while understanding the importance of designing for manufacturability from the early stages of the project.
